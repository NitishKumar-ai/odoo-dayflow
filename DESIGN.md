# Dayflow Design System

## Direction: Working Day

Dayflow should feel like a calm operating rhythm for the whole workday. The public site uses an editorial, narrative layout; the authenticated product stays disciplined and operational. The visual language is inspired by the clarity and optimism of modern HR software, without copying another brand's assets or composition.

## Identity

- Logo: **Merging Paths** — a geometric D crossed by a single horizontal flow line and a signal node. It represents employee and people-team workflows meeting in one source of truth.
- Display type: Bricolage Grotesque.
- Body and UI type: Instrument Sans.
- Data and labels: IBM Plex Mono.
- Voice: clear, warm, specific, and lightly opinionated. Prefer workday outcomes over feature inventory.

## Layout and interaction

- Use an 8px spacing base, compact 4–6px control radii, and 10–12px framed-card radii.
- Do not use decorative gradients. Favor solid color, fine rules, offset shadows, and generous whitespace.
- Marketing motion should explain flow: timeline reveals, a drawn connector, live-state pulse, and a slow process ticker.
- All motion must have a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback and content must remain available without JavaScript.
- Preserve strong type scale and hierarchy on mobile; stack narratives rather than shrinking them into dense grids.
